Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

ReLearn is a study-focused social platform. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, username, display name, chosen subject, optional bio and profile photo.
  • Content you post: photos, videos, stories, captions, comments, likes, saves, reposts, follows and direct messages.
  • Reports you send about other content or users.

How it is used

To run the service: show your profile and posts to other users, deliver messages and notifications, and keep the platform study-focused. Content is checked for appropriateness by an AI model that runs on your own device; the media is not sent to a third-party AI service.

Storage and security

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) and protected with Row Level Security so users can only change their own data. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never visible to us. Direct messages are visible only to the two participants. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not use advertising trackers. Posts, profile details, comments, likes and follows are visible to other signed-in ReLearn users.

Your rights

You can edit your profile and delete your posts at any time. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in the app under Profile → Delete account. See how to delete your account.

Children

ReLearn is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
